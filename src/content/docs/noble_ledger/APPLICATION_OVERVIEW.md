Noble Ledger supports company accounting and community management. Its workspaces cover general ledger, accounts payable and receivable, banking, reports, funds, owners, and units.

## Everyday accounting

Use **General Ledger** for journal entries and account maintenance. **Accounts Payable** groups vendors, bills, and payments; **Accounts Receivable** groups customers, assessments, invoices, and aging.

Begin with [your first journal](/general-ledger/first-journal/) or [your first vendor bill](/accounts-payable/first-bill/).

## Banking and reporting

Use **Banking** for bank feeds and [reconciliation](/banking/reconciliation/). Use **Reports** for financial statements, budget analysis, and the report library.

[Reading financial statements](/accounting/how_to_read_financial_statement/) explains the concepts behind these reports.

## Company and community

**Company** groups funds, periods, and team access. **Community** groups owners, units, and reserve studies. **System** includes documents, audit trail, projects, and settings.

Your company context and assigned access determine the actions available to you. Check the selected company before entering or reviewing data.

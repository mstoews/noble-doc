Use **Accounts Receivable → AR Aging** to identify outstanding balances and open the statement behind a total.

## Review balances

1. Confirm the company and open **AR Aging**.
2. Set the as-of date to the date you want to review.
3. Review the summary tiles: **Current (≤30 d)**, **31–60 days**, **61–90 days**, and **90+ days**.
4. Find the resident or customer in the table and review their bucket amounts and total.
5. Select the name to open the full statement. The name opens the statement as a whole, rather than filtering it to a single aging bucket.

Use the underlying charges, dates, and receipts to understand the balance before following up. The screen's “Current (≤30 d)” label should not by itself be treated as proof that every included charge is within its payment terms.

## Follow up from the statement

If a payment has arrived, [record and allocate a receipt](/accounts-receivable/record-receipt/). If a charge is missing, review the original billing record and its journal status before [creating an invoice](/accounts-receivable/first-invoice/).

An **unmatched AR customers** badge means some customer records have not resolved through the resident directory. Review the customer records before assuming the resident view includes every expected account.

For the report version, open [Report Library](/reports/report-library/) and select **AR Aging** under Receivables. Keep the review date consistent when comparing outputs.

Noble Ledger organizes work around your company and the accounting task you need to complete.

## Before you begin

Sign in using your organization's account. Confirm that you are working in the intended company before entering transactions. Your role determines which actions are available; contact your administrator if you cannot access a required screen.

## Find your workspace

The application menu groups related tasks together:

| Area | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboards | Board and fund overviews |
| General Ledger | Journal entries, templates, accounts, transfers, and close |
| Accounts Receivable | Customers, assessments, invoices, and aging |
| Accounts Payable | Vendors, bills, approvals, and payments |
| Banking | Bank feeds and reconciliation |
| Reports | Statements, budgets, and the report library |
| Community | Owners, units, and reserve studies |
| Company | Funds, periods, and team |
| System | Documents, audit trail, projects, and settings |

## Choose your first task

- [Create your first journal](/general-ledger/first-journal/) to learn draft and posting behavior.
- [Enter your first vendor bill](/accounts-payable/first-bill/) to prepare an invoice for approval.
- [Reconcile a bank account](/banking/reconciliation/) to work from a bank statement through review.

## Setting up a company

Before routine entry, review your chart of accounts, funds, fiscal periods, and team access with your administrator. The [existing setup guide](/getting_started/getting_started/) explains account mapping; its screenshots may differ from the current interface.

## Learn the accounting concepts

Start with [fund accounting](/accounting/fund_accounting/) and [reading financial statements](/accounting/how_to_read_financial_statement/). These explain the concepts that support the application workflows.

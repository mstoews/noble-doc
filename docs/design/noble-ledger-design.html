Getting started

Set up your first corporation, import balances, and post an opening entry in under ten minutes.

Create the corporation

Each condominium corporation gets its own isolated ledger. Run nbl init and Noble Ledger scaffolds a chart of accounts pre-mapped to your province's reporting requirements.

terminal
# create a corporation ledger for Saskatchewan
nbl init --corp "Maple Court CC-4821" --region SK
Note. Opening balances must reconcile to your prior auditor's closing statement before the ledger will accept its first posting.

Import opening balances

Bring in operating and reserve balances from a CSV or your previous system. Noble Ledger validates that debits equal credits before anything is committed.
